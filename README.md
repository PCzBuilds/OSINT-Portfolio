## English

### Who I Am

I am an IT Security student, approaching this field from an unconventional background. I am building practical OSINT skills with a focus on investigative research for open-source intelligence analysis.

This portfolio documents my journey from beginner to professional-grade investigator, one case and one writeup at a time.

**Focus areas:**
- Missing persons investigations
- Corruption and organised crime
- Human rights violations and documentation
- Digital rights and online accountability

---

### Principles

All investigations in this portfolio:
- Use exclusively **legal, publicly available sources**
- Follow **ethical OSINT practices** (no harassment, no doxxing, no privacy violations beyond what is justified by public interest)
- Are documented with **transparent methodology** and **verifiable sources**
- Are conducted in the spirit of **accountability journalism and human rights documentation**
