# 30 Days of OSINT

A daily open-source intelligence practice log: one write-up per day for 30 days, across three cases.

## Result

Placed **3rd out of 195 participants** in 30 days of OSINT, October 2026. The top 10 consisted of professionals in the field and advanced practitioners.

This repository starts with the three write-ups that earned bonus points, followed by the daily case write-ups.

## Goals

- Practice OSINT methodology daily and document it.
- Focus on process, verification and reasoning, not on identifying people.
- Build a reference of techniques I can reuse.

## Structure

| Folder | Content |
|--------|---------|
| `bonus-writeups/` | The three write-ups that earned bonus points |
| `case-01/` | Daily write-ups for Case 01 |
| `case-02/` | Daily write-ups for Case 02 |
| `case-03/` | Daily write-ups for Case 03 |

Each daily write-up is one file, for example `case-01/day-01.md`.

## Write-up format

Every write-up follows the same sections:

1. **Objective**: what I was trying to find out
2. **Method**: the steps and reasoning
3. **Tools**: what I used
4. **Findings (redacted)**: results, with sensitive details removed
5. **Lessons learned**: what worked, what didn't

## Ethics and redaction

- Passive, publicly available sources only. No contact or interaction with any subject.
- Names, handles, locations and other personal details of real people are redacted.
- Screenshots and files are stripped of metadata before publishing.
- I follow the rules of any organizer or platform the cases come from.

## Disclaimer

This repository is for educational purposes. It documents techniques, not individuals.
